Practice layer

When you meet a data trainer or analyst, ask three useful questions, and use them on anyone who offers to "analyse your business":

  1. What exactly will you look at in my records?
  2. What decision will the result help me make?
  3. How will you handle my customer information?

A good instructor explains in your language, uses your examples (stock, sales, M-Pesa), and shows the working. Use this session the same way: ask "so what do I do with that?" after every idea.

Level-up layer

Expertise is shown by clear explanations and checkable work, not by titles. Ask to see how a number was calculated. Be careful sharing customer names, phone numbers or M-Pesa statements with anyone, including trainers, unless you have agreed how they will be protected. Kenya's Data Protection Act applies to customer data you hold, so learn the basics. Good next step: write down the one business question you most want answered.

Check yourself: Why is "what decision will this help me make?" a useful question to ask any analyst?

Answer: It forces the analysis to be tied to action. Without a decision in mind, you get charts that look impressive but change nothing in your business.
