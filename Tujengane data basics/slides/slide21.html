Practice layer

Before joining anything, ask the organisers these questions and get clear answers:

  1. What exactly do I receive when I finish, and what are the conditions?
  2. What does "data partner" involve, and what is expected of me?
  3. What data or tools do I get access to, and who sees my information?

Write the answers down. Keep your own proof of skill too: save a sample dashboard or cleaned sheet in a folder, since a portfolio of real work speaks louder than any label.

Level-up layer

A certificate shows you attended or passed; evidence of work shows what you can do. Do both. Do not assume any outcome such as jobs or income from a programme: ask what is actually offered. Protect your personal and business information when joining groups, and avoid posting private data. Be wary of any offer that asks for payment details without clear terms in writing. Next, decide your own learning goal for the next three months.

Check yourself: Why should you keep a portfolio of your own cleaned sheets or dashboards in addition to any certificate?

Answer: It shows concrete evidence of what you can do, which clients and employers can check, while a certificate only shows participation or passing.
