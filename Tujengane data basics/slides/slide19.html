Practice layer

Data literacy is the next step after clean data. A sensible learning path:

  1. Excel: totals, sorting, filters, pivots, charts using your own sales.
  2. Power BI: connect the sheet and build a dashboard.
  3. Python: automate repeated cleanup.

Before joining, ask yourself: do I have a sheet of at least a month of real records? If yes you will learn faster. Check the session details and cost on the page, and ask in the group if anything is unclear.

Level-up layer

Learn tools in the order your problems appear: do not start Python while your sheet still has mixed date formats. Excel carries most small businesses a long way. Power BI helps when you need to share dashboards or combine several files. Whatever you choose, practise with your own data, not sample files alone. Judge any course by what you can do afterwards, such as "I can build a monthly sales pivot", not by its title.

Check yourself: Which should you learn first, Python or Excel, if your records are still in one small sheet? Why?

Answer: Excel. It handles totals, filters and pivots on a small sheet, and the habits learned there carry over to Power BI and Python later.
