Level-up layer

Joining a group helps only if you take part: ask questions, share a sanitised example. Never post customer phone numbers, M-Pesa statements or other private data in a group; blur or replace names. Learners who finish usually have a small real project; pick yours now. Treat paid options as an investment: confirm what you will do in each session before paying. Revisit today's three rules monthly.

Check yourself: Why should you remove customer names and phone numbers before sharing a sheet in a WhatsApp group?

Answer: They are personal data. Sharing them publicly can harm customers and break data protection rules; anonymise or use sample data instead.
