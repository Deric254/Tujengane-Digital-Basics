Common Mistakes

Avoid these pitfalls to keep your data clean.

  • Mixing formats (e.g., 01/01/23 vs. Jan 1, 2023)
    Causes sorting issues
  • Leaving blanks in records
    Missing data skews results
  • Duplicate entries
    Leads to incorrect totals

Practice layer

Run a mistake hunt on a sheet in four steps:

  1. Sort the Date column. Rows that jump to the bottom are usually text dates like "Jan 1".
  2. Filter Amount for blanks.
  3. Select the whole table and use Data > Remove Duplicates, choosing the columns that define a duplicate.
  4. Check totals against your till book.

Example: two rows "2023-01-02, Bags, 3000" could be a real repeat sale or a double entry. Check the receipt before deleting.

Level-up layer

Removing duplicates blindly is itself a mistake: two identical sales of the same bag on the same day may both be real. A unique receipt number separates true duplicates from repeat sales. Keep an untouched copy of the original before cleaning. Mixed formats are often caused by several people or phones entering data, so fix the process, not just the sheet.
