Practice layer

Set up a simple daily log. Columns: Date | Time | Item | Qty | Amount | Method | Customer (optional).

  1. Create the sheet once; freeze the top row (View > Freeze Panes).
  2. After each sale, or at closing, add one row.
  3. At closing compare =SUMIFS(E:E, F:F, "Cash") with the cash in your till.

Example: if the log says KES 14,200 cash but the till has 13,900, you have a KES 300 gap to investigate today, while memory is fresh.

Level-up layer

The best system is the one you actually use daily, so keep it lighter than you think necessary. Record expenses in the same sheet or a twin sheet with the same style. Do not rely on memory: end-of-week reconstruction misses things. Back up the file at least weekly. If you record customer details, keep them minimal and private. After a month, you will have enough rows for your first real analysis. Next, learn how to organise them in a proper table.

Check yourself: At closing your sheet says cash sales were KES 14,200 but your till holds KES 13,900. What do you do?

Answer: Treat the KES 300 difference as a clue: check for unrecorded expenses, change given, or missed entries, and fix the record that day.
