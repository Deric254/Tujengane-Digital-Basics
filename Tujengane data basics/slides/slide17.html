Practice layer

Work through the quiz thinking, not guessing:

  1. Duplicates: unique IDs (such as receipt numbers) are the reliable fix, because eyes miss repeats in long lists.
  2. Clean data: accurate, consistent and complete together.
  3. For each wrong option, say why it fails.

Method: read all options, eliminate the clearly wrong ones, pick the best of the rest, then explain it aloud in one sentence. If you got one wrong, re-read the matching rule slide.

Check yourself: Why is "manually check for duplicates" a weak answer for large data?

Answer: People miss repeats in long lists and it takes too long. Unique IDs and tools like Remove Duplicates scale reliably.
