Practice layer

List the data your duka produces in a normal day:

  1. Sales: item, quantity, price, payment method (cash, M-Pesa, credit).
  2. Customers: who bought, who owes.
  3. Expenses: stock purchase, transport, rent, airtime.
  4. Stock: what is left at close.

Start with a sheet of columns Date | Item | Qty | Amount | Method. After one week, sort by Item and add up Amount for each. The top three items by total are your real best sellers, which may differ from what feels busiest.

Level-up layer

You already hold data in M-Pesa statements, supplier receipts, WhatsApp orders and phone contacts. Experts gather what exists before creating new forms. Collect only what you will use: tracking twenty fields that nobody reads leads to abandoned sheets. Protect customer details, since phone numbers are personal data. Next step: choose the one question you want answered (such as "which product earns most?") and record only what answers it.

Check yourself: You track Date, Item and Amount. What single extra column would let you compare cash and M-Pesa sales?

Answer: A Payment Method column, filled with the same few words each time such as Cash, M-Pesa, Credit.
