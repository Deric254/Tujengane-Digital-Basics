Practice layer

Estimate the time your mess costs, with your own numbers:

  1. Count how many minutes you spend each week hunting for or fixing a record.
  2. Multiply by 52 weeks.
  3. Divide by 60 for hours a year.

Example: 30 minutes a week x 52 = 1,560 minutes, which is 26 hours. That is more than three working days a year spent on avoidable cleanup. Spend 15 minutes on a clean routine each week and you recover much of it.

Level-up layer

The visible cost is the time; the larger cost is the decision delayed because nobody trusts the numbers. Experts count both. Cleanup is also repetitive, which is exactly what spreadsheet tools such as filters, formulas and Power Query automate later. Do not clean the same mistake twice: find the cause (for example a free-text field) and replace it with a dropdown. Next, learn what inaccurate data costs in money.

Check yourself: Your monthly cleanup takes 2 hours. How many hours is that across a year, and what is a smarter fix than doing it forever?

Answer: 24 hours a year. A smarter fix is to stop errors at entry with templates, dropdowns and consistent rules so there is less to clean.
