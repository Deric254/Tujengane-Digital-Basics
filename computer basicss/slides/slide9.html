Browsers & Searching Smart

Browsers are your window to the web. Learn to search smarter and use tabs effectively.

  • Search tips: use quotes for exact phrases, add site: to restrict results, try keywords first.
  • Tabs: keep related tasks in grouped tabs and close what you don't need.
  • Bookmarks: save useful pages and organize them into folders.
Practice layer

Search smarter in Google or another engine:

  1. Exact phrase: "clean data in Excel".
  2. Limit to a site: site:kra.go.ke tax return.
  3. Exclude a word: excel -mac.
  4. Specific file type: filetype:pdf business plan template.

Open tabs with Ctrl+T, close with Ctrl+W, reopen a closed one with Ctrl+Shift+T. Bookmark with Ctrl+D. Result: you reach the official page faster and avoid junk. Try one search each way and compare the results to feel the difference.

Level-up layer

Top results can be adverts; look for the "Sponsored" label. Check who wrote a page and when before trusting it, and compare two sources for important facts such as tax or health. Use incognito mode for private browsing on shared computers, and sign out. Too many tabs slow the computer, so use bookmarks or a tab group. For official services, type the address yourself rather than clicking search ads.

Check yourself: You accidentally closed an important tab. What shortcut brings it back?

Answer: Ctrl + Shift + T reopens the last closed tab.
