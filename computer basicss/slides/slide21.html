Practice layer

Decide if the next course suits you using a quick self-test:

  1. Can you create and find folders? If not, practise from the Files lesson.
  2. Can you type a letter and save it as PDF?
  3. Do you have a small task in mind, such as a price list or a CV?

If you answered yes to the last one, that task becomes your course project. Check the course page and group for current dates, cost and what each session covers before you commit.

Level-up layer

Choose a course by the task you want to be able to do, such as "write a CV in Word" or "track expenses in Excel". AI previews can be useful for drafting but verify outputs, and never feed private data into public tools. Be sceptical of any promise about outcomes; judge by what you can demonstrate afterwards. Practise between sessions, since skills fade without use. Keep your folder of finished practice work.

Check yourself: What is a better way to choose a course: by its title or by a task you want to do?

Answer: By the task. A clear goal such as "write a CV in Word" lets you check whether the course covers what you actually need.
