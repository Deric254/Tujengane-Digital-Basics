Storage: Where Data Lives

Different storage fits different needs: capacity vs speed vs portability.

Local

  • HDD — cheap, large capacity
  • SSD — fast, reliable, better for OS and apps

External & Cloud

  • USB drives and external SSDs — portable backups
  • Cloud storage (Google Drive, OneDrive) — sync and access anywhere
Practice layer

Pick storage by need:

NeedChoice
Fast computerSSD
Cheap large archiveHDD or external disk
Carry/share a few filesUSB flash disk
Access anywhereCloud (Google Drive, OneDrive)

Check space: open File Explorer > This PC; the bar under each drive shows free space. Safely remove a USB: click the up arrow in the taskbar, choose the eject icon, and wait for the message that it is safe to remove. Slip: a drive that does not appear in This PC; try another USB port before assuming it is broken.

Level-up layer

Do not store everything in one place: a flash disk is easy to lose and often carries viruses between computers. Cloud sync is not a backup by itself, since deleting a file on one device may delete it everywhere (check the recycle options). Keep at least 15 to 20 percent of the drive free for the system to run well. Check free storage on your phone too. Next, learn how the internet works.

Check yourself: Why is removing a USB flash disk without "Eject" risky?

Answer: Windows may still be writing data to it. Pulling it out can corrupt files, so eject first and wait for the confirmation.
