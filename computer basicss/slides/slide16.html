Connectivity: Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth & Mobile

Learn how devices connect and share data safely and efficiently.

  • Wi‑Fi: secure networks use WPA2/WPA3; avoid open networks for sensitive work.
  • Bluetooth: pair devices for short-range wireless transfer.
  • Mobile hotspots: turn your phone into internet for your laptop carefully to save data.
Practice layer

Share your phone data as a hotspot and connect safely:

  1. On Android: Settings > Network > Hotspot & tethering > Wi-Fi hotspot; set a strong password.
  2. On your laptop click the Wi-Fi icon, choose your phone's name and enter the password.
  3. Check connection: open a website.

Pairing Bluetooth: turn it on both devices, Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device, choose it. Slip: hotspot burns data quickly; avoid large downloads and updates on it.

Level-up layer

Choose WPA2 or WPA3 security for your home router and change the default password. Public or open Wi-Fi is risky for banking or passwords; use mobile data instead, and prefer https sites. Turn off Bluetooth and hotspot when not in use, since they drain battery and widen exposure. Set your laptop to treat hotspots as metered (Settings > Network > Properties) to limit background downloads. Forget old networks you no longer use.

Check yourself: Why is it safer to use mobile data than an open cafe Wi-Fi for mobile banking?

Answer: Open Wi-Fi may let others on the network watch or tamper with traffic, or be a fake network. Mobile data is a private connection.
