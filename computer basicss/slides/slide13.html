Backups & Recovery

Backups save you from accidents. We'll show easy, reliable options.

  • 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, on 2 different media, 1 off-site (cloud).
  • Automate backups to an external drive or cloud service.
  • Test recovery: ensure you can restore files before you need them.
Practice layer

Apply 3-2-1 to your important documents:

  1. Copy 1: the original on your computer.
  2. Copy 2: an external drive or flash disk (different media).
  3. Copy 3: cloud storage (off-site), such as Google Drive or OneDrive.

Steps: copy your Business folder to the external drive, then drag it into Google Drive or turn on OneDrive folder backup. Test: open the backup copy, then restore one file to the Desktop. If it opens, the backup works. Set a weekly reminder.

Level-up layer

A backup you have never restored is only a hope. Test regularly. Keep external drives unplugged between backups so ransomware cannot encrypt them too. Sync is not backup: deletions and corruptions sync across devices. Back up phone photos and contacts too. Protect sensitive backups with a password or encryption. Decide how much work you can afford to lose, such as one day, and back up at least that often.

Check yourself: You back up to a drive that stays plugged into the same laptop. What risk remains?

Answer: Theft, fire, power surge or ransomware can destroy both. Keep one copy off-site, such as in the cloud, and unplug the drive when not backing up.
