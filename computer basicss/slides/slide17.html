Privacy & Digital Citizenship

Your digital footprint matters: manage what you share and how you behave online.

  • Think before posting—assume public permanence.
  • Review privacy settings on social accounts and apps.
  • Respect others online and report abuse; be an ethical digital citizen.
Practice layer

Do a 10-minute privacy check:

  1. Open Facebook or Instagram settings and find Privacy.
  2. Set who can see future posts to Friends, not Public.
  3. Review your profile as a stranger would see it.
  4. Check app permissions on your phone: Settings > Apps > Permissions; remove access you do not need.

Before posting, ask: would I be happy if a future employer, customer or family member saw this? If not, do not post.

Level-up layer

Photos can reveal your location, school or home, so avoid posting these details in real time. Do not share other people's photos or numbers without permission; this includes customers. Online, written words can be screenshotted and shared, so keep disputes private. Report abuse and block harassers rather than reply. Your business profile is public by design, so keep personal and business accounts separate. Check how your accounts are set up now, since options change.

Check yourself: You want to post a photo of a customer holding your product. What should you do first?

Answer: Ask for their permission, ideally in writing or message, and respect it if they say no.
