Security Basics: Stay Safe

Protect yourself: updates, antivirus, and cautious clicking go a long way.

  • Keep your OS and apps updated—patches fix vulnerabilities.
  • Install reputable security software and scan only trusted files.
  • Be cautious with links and attachments; verify sender identity.
Practice layer

Four habits that stop most attacks:

  1. Update: Settings > Windows Update > Check for updates.
  2. Check protection: Windows Security app > Virus & threat protection; it should say no action needed.
  3. Check links: hover to see the real address before clicking.
  4. Verify requests: if a message asks for money or an M-Pesa PIN, call the person on a known number.

Example: an SMS says "Your account is locked, click here". Do not click; open the official app or site yourself.

Level-up layer

Phishing works by creating urgency or fear, so slow down when a message rushes you. No genuine bank or M-Pesa agent will ask for your PIN or full password. Avoid pirated software and cracked programs, which commonly hide malware. Do not plug unknown flash disks into your computer. Use a standard user account for daily work. If you click something bad, disconnect from the internet, change passwords from a clean device and run a scan.

Check yourself: You get a message saying your M-Pesa is blocked and to click a link to unblock. What do you do?

Answer: Do not click. Treat it as a scam, and check through the official app or call the official number. Never share your PIN.
