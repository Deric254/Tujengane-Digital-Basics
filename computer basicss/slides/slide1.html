Practice layer

Start with a 5-minute readiness check on your own computer:

  1. Turn it on and sign in.
  2. Open File Explorer (Windows key + E).
  3. Open a browser and visit any website.
  4. Open Notepad or Word and type your name.

If all four worked, you are ready. If one failed, note it; the matching lesson will fix it. Do each short lesson with the computer in front of you, not just reading, since hands learn what eyes forget.

Level-up layer

Do not fear breaking the computer: ordinary clicking rarely does damage, and Ctrl+Z undoes most mistakes. The exceptions are deleting files, installing unknown software and entering passwords on strange pages, which later lessons cover. Keep a notebook of new terms. Short daily practice of 15 minutes beats weekly marathons. After this module, Word, Excel and email become much easier to pick up.

Check yourself: You have no computer at home. How can you still follow this module?

Answer: Use a shared, school, library or cyber cafe computer, or a smartphone for reading, then practise hands-on when you get access. Even short sessions help.
