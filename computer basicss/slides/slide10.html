Email: Send, Organize, Be Professional

Email remains essential. We'll cover composing, attachments, folders and polite style.

  • Subject line: clear and specific improves replies.
  • Attachments: check size and file type before sending.
  • Folders & Labels: archive, label and search to keep inbox zero possible.
Practice layer

Send a professional email in Gmail or Outlook:

  1. Click New mail/Compose.
  2. To: the address. Subject: specific, such as Invoice 0123 for January supplies.
  3. Body: greeting, one clear request, thanks, your name.
  4. Attach: the paperclip icon; check file name and size.
  5. Re-read, then Send.

You should see the message in Sent. Common slip: saying "see attached" and forgetting to attach; attach first, write after. Before sending, also check the spelling of the address, because one wrong letter sends your message to a stranger.

Level-up layer

Use Cc for people who should know and Bcc to hide recipients, such as a group of customers, so you do not expose their addresses. Reply All only when everyone needs to read. Large attachments may bounce, so send a cloud link instead. Create folders or labels and rules for regular senders. Use a professional address, not a nickname, for job and business mail. Be cautious with attachments from unknown senders.

Check yourself: You are emailing 50 customers and do not want them to see each other's addresses. Which field do you use?

Answer: Bcc. Put your own address in To and all customers in Bcc.
