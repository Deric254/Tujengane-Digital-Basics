    Install it

    1. Open your Downloads folder and double-click DericBI-Instructor-Setup.exe.

Help

Questions and fixes

How much does it cost and how do I pay?

A licence is 1,000 USD for life, or 100 USD per month. Install the app, then pay by PayPal on the renewal page and email us the proof with the activation code the app shows (M-Pesa to +254 791 360 805 also works). We reply with your personal licence. A monthly licence runs for 30 days plus 5 days to renew, then the app is view-only until you renew.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is the installer safe?

Windows shows this screen for new apps from small independent publishers that haven't built up download reputation yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. It only appears once, during installation. Only install files downloaded from this page or sent to you by us.

My licence is not accepted
  1. Copy the whole licence again, starting with DBIN1. and ending at the very last character.
  2. Don't add spaces, quotation marks or other text.
  3. Check it was made for the activation code your app shows now. If you reinstalled Windows or changed computers, the code is different: send us the new one.
  4. Still stuck? Send us a screenshot on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out.
Can I use my licence on a different computer?

A licence is made for one computer's activation code and is checked offline, so it will not work on another computer. If you get a new computer, install the app there and send us its new code.

Is my progress kept if I update or uninstall?

Yes. Your account and lesson progress live in your Windows user profile, not inside the program, so updating or uninstalling keeps them. Reinstall and sign in to carry on where you left off.

What happens when my licence ends?

The app returns to the activation screen. Your account and progress stay safely on your PC. Pay for the next period, send us the activation code shown on that screen (it is the same code as before), paste the new licence and carry on. You can also paste a renewal early, in Settings under Licence.

How do I update the app?

DericBI Instructor checks for updates when it starts and asks before downloading one. You can also open Settings and click Check for Updates, or simply download the newest installer from this page and run it over your current version.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen choose the forgot-password option, enter your email and answer the security question you set up when you created your account.

Does it run on Mac, Linux or a phone?

Not yet. DericBI Instructor needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). You can still get your licence key now and use it when you're on a Windows computer.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find DericBI Instructor and choose Uninstall.
